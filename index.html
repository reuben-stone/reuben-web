About

I'm a product engineer with more than ten years of experience building software across agencies, product teams and technology companies.

I started deep in the frontend, where I developed a strong interest in interaction, product quality and the details users actually experience. Over time my work expanded into architecture, backend services, infrastructure, internal tooling and product ownership.

More recently I've been working with AI-enabled products and exploring the systems required to make model-driven software useful beyond a chat interface: persistent state, context selection, tools, recovery, verification and human control.

I still care about the interface. I just increasingly care about everything that has to work behind it too.

Based between Portugal and the UK. Building independently and with product teams.
